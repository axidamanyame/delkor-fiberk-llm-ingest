How it works

Four steps from deposit to ownership. Same idea you know from hire purchase — built for Ghana, paid on MoMo.

01

Pick & deposit

Choose the device with an agent or on buynowpayslater.com. Pay a deposit and take it home the same day.

02

Pay easy

Daily, weekly or monthly collections on Mobile Money. Your agent reminds you — no bank queue.

03

Own it

Finish the plan and the device is yours. Your payment history stays with BuyNowPaysLater.

04

Step up

Good payers get the next phone, TV or fridge. Agents help you upgrade without starting from zero.

For customers

Own genuine phones, TVs and fridges without paying the full price today.

  • Interest-free plans up to 6 months
  • Agent visits at home or work
  • Cash on delivery and MoMo

For agents

Join the field team. You introduce customers, collect, and pick stock from the Field Stock Hub.

  • Commission on every plan you close
  • Your own customer book
  • Training from Operations Hub
